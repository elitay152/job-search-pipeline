# Explain fit using requirement-level evidence and uncertainty

Problem: lexical overlap or a model-generated score can look more authoritative than it is.

Acceptance:
- Separate required/preferred requirements, direct/transferable support, gaps and practical constraints.
- Every judgment links to a JD excerpt and supporting evidence or an explicit missing-information item.
- Persist confirmed follow-up answers; preserve unknowns and genuine gaps.
- Explain any optional summary rubric; do not imply hiring probability or ATS certification.
- Display company and role in application history, retaining stable IDs and snapshots internally.
- Separate deterministic coverage, model judgments and supplied human judgments.

References: JobPilot inventory UX; career-ops and ai-job-search evaluation structure.
