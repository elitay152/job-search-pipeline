# Adapted sources

Only the following two external projects contributed to resume_guidance.md. Their guidance was
selectively rewritten for this application's evidence and bounded-review contracts; their code,
automations and full skill collections are not installed or executed.

- [Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills), revision
  `74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d`: resume-formatter, tech-resume-optimizer,
  resume-bullet-writer. MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Resume Skills.
  License: [ResumeSkills-MIT.txt](licenses/ResumeSkills-MIT.txt).
- [varunr89/resume-tailoring-skill](https://github.com/varunr89/resume-tailoring-skill), revision
  `9a4a0f20f5983d1b533627b8c5191acd1ca0cd89`: resume-library organization, requirement matching,
  role presentation and branching experience discovery. MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Varun Ramesh.
  License: [resume-tailoring-skill-MIT.txt](licenses/resume-tailoring-skill-MIT.txt).

Intentionally excluded: invented or automatically adjusted metrics, interview/ATS predictions,
automatic title reframing, scraping, LinkedIn research, batch applications, and unrestricted iteration.
Separate user-approved descriptive titles are supported without changing official employment facts.
No material from Proficiently or Anthropic's DOCX skill is incorporated into this adaptation.
The existing Anthropic model adapter is unrelated to the excluded DOCX skill.
